Keeping your workspace clean and compliant is essential. Flex Forms provides granular control over how and when form schemas and submission data are removed.

## Deleting a Form Schema

If a form has run its course, you can permanently remove it from your Hub.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the Form">
    Navigate to the Forms Hub in your Filament panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute Deletion">
    Open the context menu (three dots) on the form card and click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Action">
    Carefully confirm the deletion prompt. 
    
    <Warning>
      **Destructive Action:** Deleting a form removes the entire Studio configuration, logic rules, and published links. This action cannot be undone.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## The Data Retention Architecture

Deleting a form schema **does not** automatically purge your historical data. Flex Forms employs a decoupled architecture to protect your respondent records.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submissions Data" icon="inbox">
    Raw submissions tied specifically to the deleted form will be orphaned or removed based on your specific database cascade configurations. Always export critical CSVs first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unified Contacts" icon="users">
    People who submitted the form are stored in the global **Contacts Directory**. Deleting the form will *not* delete these global user profiles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Compliance & Best Practices

To maintain GDPR compliance and a pristine Insights dashboard, adhere to these guidelines:

- **Export Before Destruction:** Always run a full CSV export from the Submissions tab before deleting a production form.
- **Scrub Test Data Regularly:** Frequently delete test submissions generated during your staging phase so they do not pollute your live production Insights and conversion rates.
- **Enforce Company Policy:** Manually purge the Unified Contacts directory according to your organization's legal data retention limits.
