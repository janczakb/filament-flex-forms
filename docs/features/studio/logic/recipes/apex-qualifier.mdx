**Apex Lead Qualifier** is a logic recipe for B2B qualification: budget messaging, optional “Other” company size, a calculated lead score, and High / Nurture endings.

## What you get

- **Show** a warning when budget is too low (Next/Submit stay available)
- **Show** + **Require** an “Other” text field when needed
- Score from budget / size / timeline → **Jump** to priority or nurture pages (both terminal)
- Internal lead score stays **hidden** on public Fill (used only for routing)
- Tiles stepper that only shows the visited path when branching

## How to build it

1. **Create form**, then add qualifier fields and ending pages in Studio (mark endings terminal)
2. Tune show / require and score jumps in Workflow → Rules
3. Test fill: low budget shows the warning but Next remains; high score lands on the priority page with Submit
