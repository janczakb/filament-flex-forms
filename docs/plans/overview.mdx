Plans mainly change **how many domains** you can activate, whether you can [hide branding](/plans/hide-branding), support priority, and yearly vs one-time billing.

## Purchasing workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your plan">
    Open the [Flex Forms Store](https://shop.bjanczak.com/checkout/buy/3d3a0d72-c9d5-4cfd-90c9-26869c444bb0) and choose **Standard** ($99/yr), **Pro** ($199/yr), **Enterprise** ($799/yr), or **Lifetime** ($899 once).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Composer">
    Log into the portal and copy your **Composer token** (`cmp_…`). Use it to download the package from `composer.bjanczak.com`. See [Installation](/get-started/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate domains">
    Register hostnames up to your plan’s activation limit. See [Activate license](/get-started/activate-license).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Upgrades:** Buy a higher plan in the [store](https://shop.bjanczak.com/checkout/buy/3d3a0d72-c9d5-4cfd-90c9-26869c444bb0), then open the [License Portal](https://license.bjanczak.com/flex-forms/panel) → **Change license key** and paste the new Lemon key. Hide branding and extra seats unlock on the next license check — no redeploy.
</Tip>
