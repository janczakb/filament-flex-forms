**Less than or equal** checks whether a numeric answer is **at or below** your threshold — including an exact match at the cap.

Use it for spending limits, inventory caps, “score ≤ failing threshold”, and tier ceilings. **Number fields only** — date fields use **Before** without an inclusive variant.

---

## When to choose this

| Goal | Use **Less than or equal** | Use something else instead |
|------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| At or below a number | ✓ | — |
| Strictly below (exclude boundary) | ✗ | **[Less than](/features/studio/logic/operators/lt)** |
| At or above floor | ✗ | **[Greater than or equal](/features/studio/logic/operators/gte)** |
| Date on or before | ✗ | **Before** with adjusted date on date fields |
| Exact match only | ✗ | **[Equals](/features/studio/logic/operators/eq)** |

---

## Exact behavior

- **Numbers only.**
- Blank source or comparison → never matches.
- Float comparison (`<=`); `"100"` vs `100` works.
- Pick another field in **Value** for dynamic ceilings.

---

## Which field types

| Family | Available |
|--------|-----------|
| Number | ✓ |
| Date & time | — |
| Text, choice, yes/no, presence | — |

---

## Blank / unanswered answers

**Never matches.** Add [Is empty](/features/studio/logic/operators/empty) for skipped-field paths.

## Configure in Studio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a rule">
    **Logic** → **Rules**, or **Branch** / **Add condition** on a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a number field">
    Currency, integer, rating, calculated total, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Less than or equal">
    Number family only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the cap">
    e.g. `500`, `3.5`, or pick `Max allowed` in **Value**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Then actions">
    [Show blocks](/features/studio/logic/actions/show-blocks) warnings, [Hide submit](/features/studio/logic/actions/hide-submit) when over limit (pair with inverse rule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on Fill">
    At cap, above cap, below cap, blank.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Worked examples

### Free tier limit

> **When** Team size **less than or equal** `5` → **Then** [Show blocks](/features/studio/logic/actions/show-blocks) `Free plan eligible`

Exactly 5 seats qualifies.

### Fail band

> **When** Score **less than or equal** `49` → **Then** [Jump to page](/features/studio/logic/actions/jump-to-page) `Retake`

Score 49 matches; 50 does not.

### Within approved budget

> **When** Estimated cost **less than or equal** *(pick)* `Budget cap` → **Then** enable submit path

### Inventory warning

> **When** Requested qty **less than or equal** `0` → **Then** [Hide submit](/features/studio/logic/actions/hide-submit)

Combine with validation messaging on canvas.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting **≤** on dates — not in Studio for date family.
- Using **Less than** when boundary should pass — use **≤**.
- Single rule for both over-cap and under-cap — split into two rules or use separate **Rules** rows.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Operator missing">
    Field not numeric — use integer/currency or calculated number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cap reference empty">
    Referenced field blank until filled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Need inclusive date cutoff">
    Use **Before** with (deadline + 1 day) or numeric days-until field.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Floating point surprises">
    Currency with many decimals — test realistic inputs on Fill.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
