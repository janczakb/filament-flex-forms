Flex Forms runs **inside your own Laravel application and database** — respondent submissions, Contacts, and Insights events are rows in your database, not data sent to a third-party SaaS. You are the data controller for anything your forms collect; Flex Forms is the software you use to collect and process it.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stays in your database" icon="database">
    Submissions, Contacts, Insights events, uploaded files, and Insights share tokens.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leaves your app only when you configure it to" icon="arrow-up-right">
    Email notifications, [webhooks](/developers/webhooks), and any [integration](/integrations/overview) feed you explicitly enable.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Table>
  <thead><tr><th>Data category</th><th>Where it lives</th><th>Your controls</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Submission answers</td><td>Your database (optionally encrypted at rest)</td><td>[Encrypt answers](/features/settings/general), [Data retention](/guides/access-gates), manual deletion</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Analytics beacon events (device, country, UTM)</td><td>Your database</td><td>Bot filtering is automatic; raw event retention is configurable via <code>insights.raw_retention_days</code></td></tr>
    <tr><td>Contacts (name, email)</td><td>Your database</td><td>Derived from submissions — delete the source submission to remove a contact's entries</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Uploaded files</td><td>Your configured disk (public for design assets, private for submissions)</td><td>See [Upload disks](/developers/upload-disks)</td></tr>
    <tr><td>License validation calls</td><td>Sent to the license server (domain + public key only)</td><td>Required for the plugin to function; no submission data is included</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set data retention per form">
    Turn on **Data retention** in Form settings and choose a value/unit (days/weeks/months/years) to automatically age out old submissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what you export">
    CSV / Excel exports (Submissions, [Contacts](/guides/contacts-workflow)) and [webhook](/developers/webhooks) payloads are exactly what you configure them to send — audit field selection before wiring a feed to a third party. Encrypted forms still export decrypted values to authorized Studio users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restrict access to raw files">
    Submission uploads are private and signed by default — see [Upload disks](/developers/upload-disks) and the [Multi-tenancy](/developers/multi-tenancy) recipe for `uploads.authorize`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Honor deletion requests">
    Since data lives in your own database, fulfilling a respondent's deletion request is a normal delete against your `flex_form_submissions` / Contacts rows — no separate vendor process required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Flex Forms itself only calls out to the license server to validate your domain/plan. It does not transmit form submissions, contacts, or files to any third-party service unless you explicitly configure an [integration](/integrations/overview) or [webhook](/developers/webhooks) to do so.
</Note>
