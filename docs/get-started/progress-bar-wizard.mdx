Long forms can cause drop-offs if users don't know how much is left. Flex Forms shows a **wizard stepper** on multi-page forms — either numbered tiles or a continuous progress bar.

## Branching forms (path-aware progress)

When your form uses **Jump to page** (or auto-jump funnels), progress follows the **visited path** — the pages the respondent actually opened — not every page in the Studio schema.

- **Progress bar:** fills from the visited path (so skipped branch pages do not inflate “steps left”).
- **Tiles:** pages on other branches stay **hidden** until the respondent visits them; completed path steps stay marked complete.

Linear forms without jumps keep classic step-of-N indexing.

## Enabling the Stepper

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add more than one page">
    In Studio, split your form into pages. The stepper only appears when there are **two or more** pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open General Settings">
    Open **Studio → Form settings (gear) → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a style">
    Set **Wizard stepper** to **Tiles**, **Progress bar only**, or **None**. Optionally set the stepper accent color to match your brand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices for High Conversion

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Keep Titles Concise" icon="text-size">
    Page titles render directly inside the stepper UI. Keep them short (e.g., "Personal Info" instead of "Tell us about your personal background").
  </Card>
  <Card title="Front-Load Complexity" icon="weight-hanging">
    Place harder, required questions early in the flow. This ensures the progress bar feels honest and momentum carries the user to the end.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
