Forms don't just collect abstract data; they collect people. 

The **Contacts** directory is a built-in, lightweight CRM. Every time a user completes a submission that includes their Name or Email, the Flex Forms engine automatically extracts their identity and builds a unified profile across all your forms.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Submit[User_Submits_Form] --> Extract[Engine_Extracts_Identity]
  Extract --> Contact[Profile_Created_or_Updated]
  Contact --> Directory[Global_Contacts_Directory]
  Directory --> Search[Search_and_Filter]
  Directory → Export[Export_to_CSV]
  Directory → CRM[Sync_to_External_CRM]
```

CSV export downloads as a streamed file (filters applied) so large directories do not freeze the Studio tab.

## The unified Profile

Instead of hunting through hundreds of raw submissions to find "Jane Doe", you can simply open the Contacts directory.

<Table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Profile Intelligence</th>
      <th>What it tells you</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>**Core Identity**</td>
      <td>Their Full Name and Primary Email Address.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Origin Tracking**</td>
      <td>Exactly which form (e.g., *Summer Giveaway* vs. *Contact Us*) acquired this user.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Engagement Score**</td>
      <td>The total number of times this specific human has interacted with your brand across all active forms.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Lifecycle Timestamps**</td>
      <td>When they first discovered you (*Joined At*), and the last time they interacted (*Last Seen At*).</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>

<Callout type="info">
  **Intelligent Deduplication:** If Jane Doe submits your "Contact Us" form on Monday, and your "Giveaway" form on Friday, the engine does *not* create two duplicate contacts. It intelligently updates her existing profile, incrementing her engagement score and updating her *Last Seen* timestamp.
</Callout>

## Managing Your Audience

The Contacts directory is built for speed, allowing you to instantly manipulate massive lists of leads.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lightning-Fast Search">
    Type a name or email into the search bar to instantly isolate a specific high-value lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Behavioral Sorting">
    Sort your audience by **Last Seen** to find your most active recent leads, or by **Submission Count** to identify your most loyal brand advocates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CSV Exporting">
    Click the **Export** button to instantly generate a clean CSV file of your filtered audience, ready to be dropped into Mailchimp, Excel, or a manual mail-merge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automated CRM Syncing">
    For zero-touch operations, skip manual exports entirely. Use the [Integrations Engine](/features/settings/integrations) to automatically push these contacts directly into HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zapier the millisecond they hit submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>
