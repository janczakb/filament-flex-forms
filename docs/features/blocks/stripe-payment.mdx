The **Stripe Payment** block is the only block that charges cards (and other Stripe methods) on public fill and embeds.

<Banner type="info" dismissible={false}>
  Connect keys under **Integrations → Stripe** first. Configure amount, currency, capture, and billing **on this block** — not on Form Settings → Integrations.
</Banner>

## Limits

- **At most one** Stripe Payment block per form  
- Available in the Studio catalog under **Payments** after Stripe is **Ready**  
- Works on fill + embed; skipped for real confirm in **Preview** (no charge, no transaction)

## What to configure

See the full field-by-field guide: [Stripe Integration → Payment block settings](/integrations/stripe#payment-block-settings-studio).

Quick checklist:

1. **Required** on/off (optional donations vs must-pay)  
2. **Payment type** — fixed / product options / customer amount / from field  
3. **Currency** + capture mode  
4. **Payment methods** — Automatic (Dashboard methods) or Cards only  
5. Billing / shipping / receipt email options  

## Not this block

| Block | Role |
|---|---|
| [Credit card](/features/blocks/credit-card) | Collects card-*like* text into the submission — **not** a gateway |
| [Currency](/features/blocks/currency) / [Number](/features/blocks/number) | Can feed **From a form field** amounts into Stripe Payment |

## After publish

Successful charges appear in **[Transactions](/features/transactions/overview)**. Refunds happen there (or via Stripe + webhook sync).
