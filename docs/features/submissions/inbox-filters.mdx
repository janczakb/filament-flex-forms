Understanding the state of your data is critical for accurate reporting. The Submissions Inbox uses a tabbed filtering system to instantly segment your entries based on their completion status.

## Completed vs. Partial Data

If you have enabled [Partial Submissions](/features/settings/general) in your Form Settings, Flex Forms will silently save data as the respondent types. This means the Inbox contains two distinct types of data:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Completed" icon="circle-check">
    The respondent successfully reached the end of the form and clicked the final Submit button. This is a converted lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partial (Abandoned)" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    The respondent filled out a few fields but closed their browser before finishing. This is an abandoned lead.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigating the Filter Tabs

The top of the Inbox features three quick-access tabs that control exactly what data is displayed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="All">
    Displays the raw, unfiltered firehose of data. This includes both Completed and Partial rows, giving you a complete view of all interaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed">
    The most important tab. This filters out all abandoned attempts, leaving only verified, finished conversions. **You should always use this tab before exporting data to a CRM.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Partial">
    Filters down to *only* abandoned sessions. This is a goldmine for UX research—you can look at these rows to see exactly which question caused respondents to give up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Integration Safety:** Third-party integrations (like Zapier, Webhooks, or Email Notifications) **only** trigger on Completed submissions. You never have to worry about an unfinished Partial row triggering a welcome email prematurely.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a Partial submission become Completed later?">
    **Yes.** If a respondent closes their laptop, but returns later (within the same browser session) and finishes the form, Flex Forms intelligently upgrades their existing Partial row to Completed, preventing duplicate entries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I export Partial submissions?">
    Usually, no. If you export Partial rows into your sales CRM, your sales team will waste time calling leads that have missing data. Always click the **Completed** tab before generating a CSV export for external systems.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
