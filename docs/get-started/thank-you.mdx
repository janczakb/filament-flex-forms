The moment after submission is critical. It's your opportunity to confirm receipt, set expectations, or drive the user to the next stage of your funnel. 

## Configuration Options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Success Message">
    Show a beautifully branded confirmation screen immediately after submission. 
    *Best for simple contact forms and surveys.*
    
    1. Open the Studio and select the **Thank You** node at the bottom of the canvas.
    2. Write a clear, concise headline (e.g., "We've received your request!").
    3. Add supporting text explaining the next steps (e.g., "Our team will reply within 24 hours.").
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="URL Redirect">
    Bypass the success screen and instantly send the user to another webpage. 
    *Best for continuing a complex SaaS onboarding flow or triggering a file download.*
    
    1. Open **Form Settings → Behavior**.
    2. Toggle **Redirect on Submit**.
    3. Paste your destination URL.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Design Best Practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Be Explicit" icon="check-double">
    Don't just say "Thanks." Tell them exactly what happens next. Will they get an email? Should they expect a call?
  </Card>
  <Card title="Provide an Escape" icon="door-open">
    If using the Success Message, provide a button or link back to your homepage so they aren't trapped on a dead-end screen.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
